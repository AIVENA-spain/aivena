## Valuation — what the home is actually worth and who decides

### S1 · Verified
> Your Costa Blanca home can be described by several different price instruments: portal asking prices; the Catastro's valor de referencia; actual transaction and deed evidence from the Notariado and the Registradores; INE's price index at autonomous-community level; and MIVAU appraisal values. They measure different things and must never be treated as versions of one number.

**Researches as:** For a residential property in a named Costa Blanca town, what does each published price series actually measure (asking, registered sale, appraisal), at what geography and reference period is each published, and which of them — if any — is evidence of what a buyer would pay for that specific home?

*Must establish*
- Separate four instruments and never merge them: (1) portal asking-price data, which is private and advertised, not paid; (2) the Catastro's valor de referencia, an administrative value; (3) transaction and deed evidence from the Colegio de Registradores and the Consejo General del Notariado, which is what buyers actually paid; and (4) MIVAU valor tasado, which is an APPRAISAL series and is never a record of what anyone paid.
- Establish, for the specific town the piece names, whether any of these instruments publishes a figure at municipal level at all, and if not, name the smallest geography for which a figure exists and say so in the copy.
- Determine, for each figure taken from a press release, whether 'Alicante' in that release means the city or the province, and confirm it against the publisher's own page rather than a search summary or aggregator.

*Never assume*
- That a province-wide figure describes any particular town, or that a town figure can be inferred from the province one.
- That 'Alicante' in a portal press release means the province — it commonly means the city, which is not a coastal-resort market.
- That asking price, registered sale price and appraisal value are versions of one number, or that the difference between two of them measures anything.

### S2 · Verified
> The pool, the glazed terrace, the extra bedroom: if the register has never heard of them, find out what they do to your price before the buyer's valuer does

**Researches as:** Where a Costa Blanca home has built area on the ground that does not appear in the Registro de la Propiedad or the Catastro, what effect does that have on a mortgage valuation, on the buyer's finance and on the notarial deed — and what routes exist to regularise it before a sale?

*Must establish*
- Establish what a declaración de obra nueva terminada requires under art. 28.1 of the consolidated Texto Refundido de la Ley de Suelo y Rehabilitación Urbana (RDLeg 7/2015) — certificación de técnico competente acreditativa de la finalización conforme al proyecto, proof of the requisitos de la legislación de la edificación for handover, and the administrative authorisations (or the acreditación that a comunicación previa / declaración responsable was filed and its period ran) — and separately what the obra nueva por antigüedad route requires under art. 28.4 TRLSRU, together with the grounds on which a Registrador must refuse it: an anotación preventiva for a live expediente de disciplina urbanística, suelo de carácter demanial, or suelo affected by servidumbres de uso público general. There is no single 'planning text': the state gateway is art. 28 TRLSRU, and the Valencian layer that fixes the 15-year restoration-of-legality clock is the TRLOTUP (Decreto Legislativo 1/2021), not Andalusia's AFO.
- Establish whether and on what basis a mortgage valuer may include square metres that appear on neither the register nor the Catastro — do not infer this from what agents say.
- Establish whether an unregistered extension prevents the notary from authorising the deed, or only affects price, finance and the buyer's own risk; if the answer is not established from a primary source, say in the copy that it is not.

*Never assume*
- That an undeclared extension makes the sale illegal or impossible — legality of the works, registrability of the title and saleability are three different questions.
- That the antigüedad route is available for every property; land classification and public-use servitudes can exclude it, and a planning-discipline entry on the register can block it.
- That Catastro area matching the deed means the works were lawfully authorised, or that a Catastro entry regularises anything.

## Taxes on selling — CGT, plusvalía municipal, the 3% retention

### S9 · Verified
> Selling your Costa Blanca home while non-resident for Spanish tax purposes? The buyer must hand 3% of the price straight to Hacienda instead of to you — but it is a payment on account of your capital-gains tax, not the tax itself: once you file your modelo 210 you may get all of it back, some of it, or none, and you may still owe more. Here is who takes it, why, and how you settle it afterwards — whether any of it comes back depends on the gain.

**Researches as:** When a property in Alicante province is sold by a seller who is not a Spanish tax resident, what withholding obligation arises, who is legally obliged to carry it out, what form and deadline apply to it, and by what procedure and timeline can the seller reclaim any amount withheld above the tax actually due?

*Must establish*
- Establish which statute imposes the withholding on transfers by non-resident sellers, whether the obligation falls on the buyer or the seller, and whether it is characterised as a final tax or a payment on account
- Establish which form the withholding is paid on, which form the seller uses to settle the actual liability, and the deadline running on each — and establish whether those deadlines are confirmed against a primary instrument or only secondary sources
- Establish whether the withholding percentage is applied to the sale price or to the gain, and what happens when the tax actually due is lower than the amount withheld, or when the sale produced a loss

*Never assume*
- That the withheld percentage equals the tax owed — the two are computed on entirely different bases, and one may be larger or smaller than the other
- That the seller controls the withholding — the obligation and the filing sit with the other side of the transaction, which is why a seller can be exposed by a counterparty's failure
- That a refund is automatic — establish whether it requires an affirmative filing by the seller, and do not state a refund waiting time unless a source gives one

## Net proceeds — what actually lands in the account

### S17 · Verified
