*Must establish*
- The legal basis in the IRNR (art. 24.5 TRLIRNR, by reference to art. 85 LIRPF) and the imputed base: 1.1% or 2% of cadastral value. Which coefficient applies turns on WHEN the municipality's cadastral values were last revised, modified or determined by a general collective valuation procedure — 1.1% if that took effect in the tax period or the 10 preceding tax periods, 2% otherwise — and not on the size of the property's own cadastral value, nor on a rate each municipality chooses. Where there is no cadastral value or it has not been notified by 31 December, the base is 1.1% applied to 50% of the greater of the acquisition price or the administratively checked value. For 2026 the ordinary 10-year rule governs: the 'revised since 1 January 2012' cut-off in DA 55ª LIRPF was written for 2023, 2024 and 2025 and must not be carried into 2026 without checking. Establish this for the 2026 tax year SPECIFICALLY: art. 85 LIRPF sets the imputation at 2% of cadastral value, dropping to 1.1% only where a general collective cadastral revision took effect in the tax period itself or in the ten preceding tax periods. The friendlier 'revised since 1 January 2012' cut-off sits in DA 55ª LIRPF, and as at 1 September 2026 the consolidated text of DA 55ª in force covers the 2023 tax period ONLY. It was not extended to 2026 — and the 2024/2025 extensions were themselves annulled: Congress derogated RDL 16/2025 on 27 January 2026 (BOE-A-2026-2024) and RDL 2/2026 on 26 February 2026 (BOE-A-2026-4667). For 2026, apply art. 85's ten-preceding-tax-periods test, not the 2012 date.
- 19% where the owner is TAX-RESIDENT in another EU Member State or in an EEA State covered by mutual assistance on exchange of tax information — which AEAT confirms means Iceland, Norway and Liechtenstein. 24% for everyone else, including someone tax-resident in the UK after Brexit.
- That the tax is self-assessed on Modelo 210 with no assessment issued, and the current filing window as amended by Orden HAC/623/2026: for 2025 imputed income, 1 January to 31 December 2026; from the 2026 accrual year onward, 1 April to 31 December of the following calendar year — so 2026 imputed income is filed between 1 April and 31 December 2027

*Never assume*
- There is no routine annual assessment bill: the owner normally self-assesses the liability on Modelo 210. Non-compliance can still bring an AEAT assessment or enforcement, so never say nothing is ever sent.
