Must establish

Which coefficient applies turns on WHEN the municipality's cadastral values were last revised, modified or determined by a general collective valuation procedure — 1.1% if that took effect in the tax period or the 10 preceding tax periods, 2% otherwise — and not on the size of the property's own cadastral value, nor on a rate each municipality chooses. Where there is no cadastral value or it has not been notified by 31 December, the base is 1.1% applied to 50% of the greater of the acquisition price or the administratively checked value. For 2026 the ordinary 10-year rule governs: the 'revised since 1 January 2012' cut-off in DA 55ª LIRPF was written for 2023, 2024 and 2025 and must not be carried into 2026 without checking. Establish this for the 2026 tax year SPECIFICALLY: art. 85 LIRPF sets the imputation at 2% of cadastral value, dropping to 1.1% only where a general collective cadastral revision took effect in the tax period itself or in the ten preceding tax periods. The friendlier 'revised since 1 January 2012' cut-off sits in DA 55ª LIRPF, and as at 1 September 2026 the consolidated text of DA 55ª in force covers the 2023 tax period ONLY.
